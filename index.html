How these four scores work

They are simple 0 to 100 scores of my own design, not medical measures. Strength: your gym sessions over the last 4 weeks against your weekly goal. Movement: your average steps against your target (70%) and mobility sessions against your goal (30%). Recovery: your average sleep, where 5 hours scores 0 and 7 hours scores 100 (70%), and how regular your bedtime is (30%). Mind: your own energy and calm check-ins over the last 4 weeks.

This week stretch time per area

Flexibility research suggests at least 5 minutes of stretching per muscle group per week, spread over 5 or more days. Short and often beats long and rare.
